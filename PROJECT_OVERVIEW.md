# Project Overview

## Business Problem

Hospital financial information may be spread across patient-payment, insurance, reimbursement, expense, and receivables datasets. This makes it difficult for management to get a single view of cash flow and financial performance.

## Objective

Build an interactive Power BI dashboard that combines multiple hospital-finance datasets into a unified analytical view.

## Project Workflow

1. Import multiple hospital finance datasets
2. Clean and transform data using Power Query
3. Build a structured data model
4. Create a Date Dimension for time-based analysis
5. Create financial KPIs and measures
6. Analyze revenue, expenses, receivables, and payments
7. Monitor insurance and government reimbursements
8. Identify cash-flow gaps and delayed payments
9. Present insights through interactive Power BI visuals

## Main Business Questions

- How much money is flowing into and out of the hospital?
- What are the major sources of revenue?
- How much is still outstanding?
- Are insurance or government reimbursements delayed?
- How are revenue and expenses changing over time?
- Is the hospital improving or weakening financially?
